# Saudi Aramco: Financial Performance & Ratio Analysis (2022 vs 2023)

**Principles of Financial Management (FIN 1202) · University of Tabuk · team project**

Analysis of Saudi Aramco's consolidated financial statements for 2022 and 2023 using common-size statements and financial ratios. Source: Aramco's published annual financial statements (amounts in SAR millions).

## What's inside
- `aramco_financial_statements_2022_2023.xlsx`: income statement and balance sheet, common-size analysis and ratio calculations

## Highlights
| Metric | 2022 | 2023 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue (SAR m) | 2,006,955 | 1,653,281 | −17.6% |
| Net income (SAR m) | 604,005 | 454,764 | −24.7% |
| Net margin (on revenue) | 30.1% | 27.5% | −2.6 pts |

## Ratios covered
- **Liquidity:** current ratio, quick ratio
- **Solvency:** debt ratio, times interest earned
- **Asset management:** inventory turnover, receivables turnover
- **Profitability:** profit margin, ROA, ROE

## Takeaway
Lower oil prices pushed revenue and profit down in 2023, but Aramco's short-term liquidity stayed strong and its reliance on debt kept falling. The key issue for management is operating efficiency while revenue is under pressure.
